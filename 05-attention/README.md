# Attention and transformers (UDL, chapter 12)

My worked versions of the chapter 12 notebooks from Simon Prince's *Understanding Deep Learning*
([udlbook.github.io](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/)): the "TODO" cells filled in and run.

| Notebook | What it builds |
|---|---|
| `12_1_Self_Attention.ipynb` | Self-attention from scratch: queries, keys, values, and the softmax over dot products |
| `12_2_Multihead_Self_Attention.ipynb` | Several attention heads in parallel, then combined |
| `12_3_Tokenization.ipynb` | Byte-pair encoding, building a vocabulary from text |
| `12_4_Decoding_Strategies.ipynb` | Greedy, beam search, top-k and nucleus sampling from a transformer |

The notebooks and exercises are the book's; the filled-in answers are mine.
